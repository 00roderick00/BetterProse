# Limitations

BetterProse is an early formative tool. It is not validated for admissions,
employment, publication, discipline, or autonomous grading.

## Do not use it to determine authorship

Writing style cannot establish whether a person used AI. BetterProse has no
detector and will not add one.

## Offline findings are narrow

The deterministic provider sees surface structure, not the truth of claims or
the quality of thought. Low-confidence scores are useful for exercising the
pipeline and locating possible revision questions, not certifying prose
quality.

## Model-assisted findings can be wrong

Models can overvalue polish, miss reasoning problems, flatten genre
differences, quote passages inaccurately, and express unstable judgments with
unwarranted confidence. Writers and instructors should inspect the cited text.

## Integrity checks are incomplete

Fact lock compares claim-surface items such as numbers, quotations, URLs, and
citations. It does not prove that retained claims are true or that an
apparently unchanged paraphrase preserves meaning.

## Fairness requires local validation

Do not equate one prestige dialect with better reasoning. Any consequential
deployment must be tested across dialects, multilingual writing, disability-
related composing practices, genres, disciplines, topics, and document
lengths.

## Scores are not institutional grades

The 0–100 total summarizes the selected rubric. Institutions must calibrate
their own anchors and performance boundaries before mapping results to grades.
