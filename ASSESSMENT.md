# ASSESSMENT

The BetterProse protocol. An assessment is not a rewrite with commentary; it is a ledger of evidence, and the rewrite is what the ledger justifies. Every edit is transparent, attributable to a rule, and reversible by anyone who disagrees with the rule.

## The ledger

One row per proposed edit:

| # | Quoted span | Pattern | Proposed rewrite | Why this is better prose |
|---|-------------|---------|------------------|--------------------------|
| 1 | "Experts warn that the strait is vulnerable" | BP-01 | "Lloyd's has reclassified the strait as high risk" | Names the actor; the claim now has an owner and a consequence. |

- **Quoted span**: the exact words from the source, quoted, not paraphrased.
- **Pattern**: a rule ID from PATTERNS.md (BP-nn or BPV-nn). Precedence entries (P-1 to P-4) may be cited alongside a rule to explain why an apparent fault was left alone.
- **Proposed rewrite**: the replacement text, or "cut."
- **Why**: one line answering "why is this better prose," never "why does this look less machine-made."

Spans the assessor considered and deliberately left alone (a placed hedge under P-2, a repeated coinage under P-1) may be recorded as no-edit rows; this keeps the reasoning inspectable.

## Three hard rules

1. **No edit without a pattern ID.** An edit that cannot cite a rule is a taste preference, and taste preferences do not go in the ledger. If a real fault has no rule, the fix is to propose a rule, not to smuggle the edit.
2. **No invention.** No rewrite introduces any fact, name, number, date, or citation absent from the source. Where the source is vague, the rewrite is vague or shorter: omission over invention (the Buff Box standard). A rewrite that sounds better because it fabricated a specific is a defect, not an improvement.
3. **Coinages survive verbatim.** Coined concepts (BPV-01) pass through every edit unparaphrased, unvaried, and repeated exactly as the author wrote them.

## Self-check

Before delivering the final text:

1. **Read it aloud** (or as near as the medium allows) for rhythm: flat stretches fail BP-14, engineered drama fails BP-16, and clustered asides fail BP-15.
2. **Confirm zero invented facts**: every name, number, date and citation in the final traces to the source.
3. **Confirm register consistency**: the final is Register A or Register B throughout (see PATTERNS.md Part II), and every coinage appears verbatim.

A final that fails any check goes back through the ledger, not around it.
